## Two halves

**Visibility in AI answers** (left) comes from DataForSEO and needs a key and a project domain. It covers the two engines DataForSEO tracks: **ChatGPT** and **Google AI** (AI Overviews and AI Mode). ChatGPT data is only available for US English; Google AI follows your project's location and language.

**Traffic from AI answers** (right) comes from your own OpenPanel data: sessions whose referrer is an AI assistant. It works without DataForSEO and covers more assistants, including Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, You.com, Grok, Mistral and Kagi.

If DataForSEO is not connected the left half and the prompt explorer stay hidden and the tab shows the traffic half alone.

## Visibility in AI answers

- **Engine cards** — how many AI answers mention your domain, per engine and in total, with the estimated monthly volume of the prompts behind them.
- **Share of voice** — your mentions against each competitor from **Settings → DataForSEO**, as a share of the group's total. Without competitors your domain simply shows 100%.
- **Top cited pages** — the pages on your site that AI answers link to most, with a per-engine breakdown.

These lookups are cached for 24 hours.

## Traffic from AI answers

- The total number of sessions referred by AI assistants in the selected range, with the change against the previous period and a daily trend.
- A table by assistant with sessions, the previous period's sessions and the change.

Use the range picker to change the window; it applies to this half only, since the DataForSEO side reports current visibility rather than a date range.

## Prompt explorer

Type a question a customer might ask an assistant, choose engines, and click **Explore**. You get:

- **Cited domains** — every site the answers cite, ranked by how many answers cite it. Your own domain is highlighted and configured competitors are marked.
- **Answers** — the matching prompts with their estimated volume, engine and number of sources. Answers that cite your site are marked *cites you*.

<Callout>Search Console has no data about AI assistants, so the traffic half is always based on OpenPanel sessions. Make sure the OpenPanel SDK runs on the pages you care about.</Callout>
