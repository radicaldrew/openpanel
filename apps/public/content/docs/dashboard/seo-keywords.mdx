## Pick a source

Enter a seed keyword on the left and choose where the results come from:

| Source | What it returns |
|---|---|
| **Ideas** | Keywords that share the topic of your seed. The broadest list. |
| **Suggestions** | Longer queries that contain your seed. |
| **Related** | Keywords that appear next to your seed in *searches related to*. |
| **Ranked keywords** | Every keyword a domain already ranks for. Pick your own domain or one of your configured competitors. |
| **From Search Console** | Your top queries for the selected range, enriched with volume and difficulty. Needs Search Console connected. |

Results use the project's location and language from **Settings → DataForSEO**, and a lookup is remembered for 24 hours, so refining the same seed does not cost again.

## The results table

Each row shows the keyword, monthly **volume**, **difficulty** (0–100), **CPC**, search **intent** (informational, commercial, transactional or navigational) and a twelve-month **trend** sparkline. The ranked-keywords source adds the ranking position and URL; the Search Console source adds clicks, impressions and average position.

Sort by any column, select rows with the checkboxes, then:

- **Track selected** adds the keywords to the [Rankings](/docs/dashboard/seo-rankings) tab.
- **Export CSV** downloads the selection, or the whole table when nothing is selected.

## SERP preview

Click a row to open the SERP preview: the top ten organic results for that keyword in your market, with your own domain highlighted, and the features Google shows on that page such as *People also ask*, video or shopping. A preview is cached for six hours.

<Callout>Search Console rows without metrics show as pending while OpenPanel fetches them in the background. They fill in on their own.</Callout>
