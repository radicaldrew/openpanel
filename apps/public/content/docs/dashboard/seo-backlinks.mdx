## Cards

The top row shows, for the selected target:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Backlinks** | Total live links pointing at the domain. |
| **Referring domains** | Distinct sites those links come from. |
| **Domain rank** | DataForSEO's authority score for the domain; higher is better. |
| **Spam score** | 0–100; higher means more of the linking sites look spammy. |
| **New / lost (30 days)** | Links gained and lost over the last month, from the history. |

For your own domain the numbers come from a stored **snapshot**. A snapshot is taken on the **backlink schedule** in Settings → DataForSEO (weekly by default) and whenever you click **Snapshot now**. If the stored snapshot is older than a day when you open the tab, OpenPanel refreshes it for you, provided you can edit the project.

## History

The chart plots the snapshots over the selected range: backlinks and referring domains, with new and lost links per day. The longer snapshots have been running, the longer the series.

## Tables

Three tabs, each paginated 50 at a time and cached for six hours:

- **Backlinks** — the linking page, anchor text, dofollow or nofollow, link type, ranks of the linking page and domain, spam score, first seen and, for lost links, when they were lost.
- **Referring domains** — one row per linking site with its backlink count, referring pages, rank, spam score and first seen.
- **Top pages** — your pages with the most backlinks and referring domains.

Filters: **dofollow / nofollow**, **status** (live, new, lost, or all), a **minimum rank**, a search box, and **hide spam** to drop links with a spam score above 40. Sort by any numeric column.

## Targets

The domain switcher offers your own domain and every competitor from **Settings → DataForSEO**. A competitor's cards and history are fetched live (and cached for six hours) rather than stored, so there is no *Snapshot now* for them. Domains outside that list cannot be looked up, which keeps one project from spending the organization's balance on arbitrary sites.
